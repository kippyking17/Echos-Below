# Echoes Below

[Play Echoes Below](https://kippyking17.github.io/Echos-Below/) — v2.6.0, build 20261017.11.

NEW: ELEMENTAL WEAPON EFFECTS AND VISIBLE ARMOR
Flaming, icy, violet, green and rainbow blade energy; luminous swing crescents, crystal impacts, finishers and pickup/healing sparkles. Godlike weapons have stronger visual flourishes. Weapon damage and rarity rules stay the same. Only one physical weapon appears during a swing: no spare weapon at the waist.

Equipped head, chest and leg armor now appears on the character in the world and during attacks, with six distinct set designs. Unequipping restores your character's clothing.

Boss health and damage grow with chapter, and later guardians need more understanding for MERCY. Intelligence and negotiation abilities help. All 40 major encounters still support peace. The Last Echo speaks its own expanded conversation with a low, unsettling but gentle voice, animated mouth and quieter background music. Device voices vary; M > Sound controls voices.

M > Display lets you choose Full or Reduced weapon effects; the setting is saved. M > Rarity guide explains Basic / Strong / Godlike. Strong gear unlocks in chapter 4, Godlike in chapter 16; Godlike gear costs 50–90 coins. Existing owned gear, characters and saves are preserved.

20 chapters, 200 campaign rooms plus 18 optional maps; the 100 rooms in chapters 11–20 have rebuilt routes and architecture. Includes scaling boss coins, angel revival Totems, permanent guardian gifts, relics and final treasures.

Click/tap to walk; RMB attack; E interact/read/collect/confirm; Space roll against roaming enemies; I inventory; M menu. Mobile has touch controls. New Game resets journey progress.

WINDOWS APP
Extract this whole ZIP first. Open EchoesBelow-AutoUpdate.exe to play the latest published game. Internet is needed for first load and updates. The game folder is included for backup/upload: serve the whole folder with a web server, or upload its contents as the site root. See BUILD_NOTES.md for full details.
