ECHOS BELOW — ONE-TIME MULTIPLAYER ACTIVATION

The game UI and co-op backend are included. For a new Supabase project, run this migration once.
The existing live project already has it installed.

1. Open your existing project's SQL Editor:
   https://supabase.com/dashboard/project/vnqjhftkaafzrrfytfoy/sql/new
2. Open multiplayer-setup.sql from this game folder. Copy ALL its text.
3. Paste into the SQL Editor and click Run. Wait for success.
4. Reload Echos Below, log in, then choose M > Journey > Multiplayer.
5. Create a private room. Share the ten-character code with up to three
   other signed-in players. Everyone clicks Ready; the leader begins.

This adds a private schema and one authenticated RPC. It does not change
your login configuration, admin authorization, or single-player saves.
Never paste database passwords, service-role keys, or secret keys into
cloud-config.json or the game files. Its existing publishable key stays as is.

CO-OP RULES
- Separate journey, beginning at chapter 1. Solo saves are untouched.
- Up to four players. No public room listing or anonymous multiplayer.
- Shared room, boss HP, ACT progress and phase. Individual player HP.
- Boss HP: solo amount x (1 + 0.55 per additional starting player).
  Disconnecting does not reset or increase/decrease the current boss HP.
- Real boss has two phases; Last Echo has three, becoming more aggressive.
- Click/tap to move, RMB or Attack to swing near an enemy. ACT also works.
- Bright danger areas warn for 1.4 seconds. Move clear or use Dodge.
- E or Revive near a downed friend restores 35% health. Dawnkeeper adds 6 HP.
- If everyone falls, the leader can retry the current fight.
- Everyone standing reaches the door; the leader uses Next door or E.
- Cleared guardian rooms have healing Graces. Victories grant permanent
  HP/attack/defense and levels for the party, once per encounter.
- The oldest remaining member becomes leader if the leader leaves.
- Refreshing can resume your current room. Disconnected slots expire
  after 35 seconds without contact. Join only while the room is a lobby.
- Room chat is limited to members, 180 characters, one message/1.5 seconds.
- Common English profanity and sexual terms are filtered on the server,
  including common leetspeak and spaced-out spellings. Offending messages
  are never delivered. The account is removed and barred from multiplayer
  for 60 seconds; refreshing, leaving or a new room cannot bypass it.
  Unsuitable account names also trigger the same ban. Word boundaries
  protect common harmless words such as class and hello. This is a word
  filter, not a guarantee of understanding every language or possible evasion.
