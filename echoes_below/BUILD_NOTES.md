# Echoes Below 2.1.0 — The Lantern Road
Build 20261017.04

The game is now entirely 2D. The first-person renderer, camera controls, Three.js dependency and cave image assets have been removed. Click/tap walks around obstacles. Right-click swings fists or an equipped weapon; I opens/closes the inventory. Mobile has an Attack button and joystick/direct heart dragging for battle dodging. Control teaching stays in How to Play and Controls.

Every new journey chooses one of six cosmetic travelers. A New Game resets the selected journey's chapter, choices, coins, supplies, equipment, bag, potions, buffs, grace gifts, enemy clears, shop rolls and grace anchor. Other save slots and account/device preferences remain intact. Saved travelers retain their appearance on reload. The title's on-screen ENTER button begins its chime, growth and fade entrance; keyboard confirmation and background clicks do not bypass it.

Graces are floating green angels, six rooms apart at each chapter entrance. No grace appears in a boss room or the room immediately before one. The final angel uses all chapter colors. Resting heals, saves and anchors respawn there. Death returns to the last rested grace, retaining collected equipment and story progress. Before any grace has been rested at, a fresh traveler returns to the beginning. Old saves recover an anchor from the chapter of their existing saved checkpoint.

Each grace offers one permanent gift for that chapter: Damage +2; Hearts +2 maximum HP; Intelligence +1 in chapters 1–10 or +2 in chapters 11–20; Defense +1 with minimum incoming damage 1; Healing +2 for berries/tonics; Movement +3%, up to a 30% grace bonus. Intelligence increases ACT understanding by 10% per point (up to double), shortens attack waves by 1% per point (up to 20%) and strengthens all six learned skills. How to Play explains every gift. Gifts cannot be farmed by revisiting a grace.

The pack has ten slots: a permanent glowberry pouch and nine flexible item/stack slots. Potions stack to nine. Equipped weapon, head, chest and legs have four separate slots. Gear purchases equip immediately and return replaced gear to the bag. Full bags block purchases/unequipping without charging coins. Inventory can equip, unequip, drink/eat or discard. Glow Tonic heals; Stonebrew prepares a shield; Clarity Draught helps the next negotiation. The battle ITEM action opens the pack when potions are carried. Quest keys use no bag slot.

Brindle is an animated shopkeeper with a moving portrait, idle/waving animation and spoken conversations. He speaks purchase thanks without printing those words. Speech uses the device's built-in speech synthesis; voice availability depends on the browser/device. Other characters keep their story boxes. Music lowers while he speaks. Shops remain in the fifth room of chapters 3, 6, 9, 12, 15 and 18.

Equipment has exactly three rarity labels: Basic (2 coins), Strong (5) and Godlike (8), before the optional relic discount. Stock is rolled once and saved per shop/chapter; later chapters improve the odds of rarer stock. Goblins may leave equipment, with better rarity odds later. New games reroll shop stock. Normal field finds are exactly two glowberries and two loose coins in every block of eight main rooms. New travelers start with no berries. Reading memories, chapter changes and minibosses no longer grant berries. Bosses/minibosses award exactly five coins; ordinary foes/goblins award none. Optional relic discoveries keep their separate one-time treasure rewards. Existing saved supplies are preserved.

Twenty chapter-themed goblin types roam selected ordinary rooms. Their health, speed and damage rise by chapter. Kills persist, and full bags leave dropped gear available to collect later. Fists and all six weapon types have distinct visible strikes, lighting and crystal impact particles. Boss strikes show the traveler and guardian in the scene while hiding the heart arena; the arena returns for dodging and other actions. Boss health rises from 42 by 5 per chapter, and projectile damage rises with chapter and phase. Existing readable warnings and peaceful routes remain.

Defeated bosses/minibosses fall and leave smoke and a passage key. Spared guardians also leave a key. The door waits for the key; it cannot fill the bag or be discarded. Seven additional chapter passage locks retain their reachable keys and permanent unlocks. Backtracking remains available. Printed solution sequences and answer-revealing hints have been removed from campaign mechanisms and optional trails; environmental stories provide indirect clues instead.

Twenty original synthesizer themes cover the twenty chapters, with distinct melodies, tempo, register and accompaniment. Each has a combat arrangement. Music is local and works without streamed audio, with its preference in the Sound menu. The finale keeps its credits theme.

Existing format-4 saves gain safe defaults for new fields and migrate legacy equipped weapons/chest armor. Cloud configuration, account authorization and the verified KingKippy admin gate are unchanged. No database migration is needed. The existing Windows launcher receives the new website build.

Validation: all campaign rooms/puzzles and optional trails; safe map paths; equipment and save migration; bag capacity/swaps; grace gifts and respawn; fresh resets across six avatars; resource spacing; rarity probabilities/prices; boss health/damage; chapter goblin identities and persistent melee kills; strike/fall/key timing; all twenty bosses and six minis on peaceful/fight routes; desktop/phone character, inventory, shop voice calls, right-click/mobile attacks and key pickup; 20 unique music themes with bounded note levels and frequencies. Mobile checks use browser device emulation.
