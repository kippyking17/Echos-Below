Colors, floating wings and halo are preserved.

Phase two adds two damage. Miniboss and ordinary enemies have gentler matching curves. The last boss has 609 HP, 13 armor and 64 base damage: its first hit defeats a fresh 24-HP traveler; a developed mixed build survives and its upgraded weapon penetrates the armor. Projectiles retain fair edge warnings and dodge gaps. Best weapons provide +28 attack; best head/chest/leg gear together provides 28 defense before grace gifts. Rarity remains Basic/Strong/Godlike, prices 2/5/8, and later chapters improve RNG odds.

E is the remappable Collect action for nearby keys, coins, glowberries and equipment. Walking or clicking an item does not collect it. Reading a landmark does not take its key. Mobile has a Collect button, enabled in range. Left click/tap moves, right click swings, I opens the ten-slot pack. Collection, repeat prevention, full bags and major-encounter keys persist through saves. Two loose coins and two berries per eight campaign rooms now yield fifty of each across the campaign.

Save format remains 4 and uses the same local keys/cloud table. campaignRooms:10 marks the expanded layout. Old six-room positions map [0,1,2,3,4,5] to [0,1,2,3,5,9]. Old midpoint encounters move to room 5. Story memories, seen rooms, puzzle flags, kills, keys, dropped loot, checkpoints and expedition return points migrate once. Authentication, cloud configuration and verified KingKippy admin authorization remain unchanged.

## Crystal & Steel — weapons and sound

Seven distinct styles cover bare hands and all six weapons, in both the world and boss battles. Fists have jabs/uppercuts and a knuckle burst; Lantern Hook sweeps through embers; Glass Saber crosses crystal cuts; Ember Staff spins into cinders; Moon Needle lunges through a constellation pierce; Chorus Spear thrusts through echo rings; Dawnkeeper cleaves with rainbow trails. Three swing variations, afterimages, character lunges, contact recoil, damage numbers and larger lethal bursts add motion. Defeated goblins fall and fade into each weapon's particles/smoke; bosses keep their falling animation, weapon finisher and passage-key drop. Transient effects are capped at 24 and expire within 1.2 seconds. Opening another room clears them. World impact stays at 180 ms in a 440-ms swing; battle impact stays at 340 ms in a 950-ms strike. Damage, reach, chapter scaling and rewards are unchanged.

Forty-two original synthesized sound categories cover weapon swings, impacts, kills, footsteps, walk targets, ACT, dialogue advances, menus, pickups, graces, upgrades, doors, puzzles, shops, purchases, equipping, healing, potions, skills, enemy alerts/warnings, player hurt, shields, death, respawn, mercy, saves, loads, portals, relics, chapter changes, title entrance and victory/credits. Each weapon has distinct swing/hit/kill pitches and textures. Effects happen on the action/impact, not in the draw loop. Frequent cues are throttled. No remote audio assets or extra downloads are needed.

Sound menu: independent music and effects mute, volume sliders and a preview. Preferences persist under echoesBelow_audio_v1. Muting music leaves effects available; muting effects also silences the title effect. A single Web Audio context uses independent gain buses through a compressor, with at most 64 effect voices. Sources and nodes disconnect on completion. The audio context starts from player input; browsers without audio support remain playable. Music and effects duck while Brindle is speaking; his device voice remains the only spoken character dialogue.
