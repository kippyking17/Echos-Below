# Echoes Below 2.2.0 — The Twenty Districts

Build 20261017.05. Twenty chapters, ten rooms each: 200 distinct campaign rooms and the existing eighteen optional trail maps. Eighty new authored memories expand Mara’s investigation, the missing volunteers, Wick’s past with Neri, the emergency that became permanent, and the districts’ shared return circuit. Original memories and indirect mechanism clues remain; journal and credits now account for 200 campaign memories.

Every chapter has an ordinary encounter in room 3, a unique named miniboss in room 5 and its main guardian in room 10. All forty major encounters support combat and mercy, award five coins once, and leave a passage key. Physical defeats retain fall/smoke animations. All 199 backward links include chapter boundaries. Shops move to room 6 of every third chapter, after the midpoint guardian. Optional trail entrances remain in room 2.

Regular Graces share room 5 with minibosses. Angel Graces share room 10 with main bosses. Both heal, save and anchor death respawn to that exact room. Each grace grants its own permanent choice once; rest and healing remain repeatable. Regular gifts: damage +2, maximum HP +2, Intelligence +1 (chapters 1–10) or +2 (11–20), defense +1, healing +2, movement +3%. Angel gifts improve those to +3 damage/HP/healing, +2–3 Intelligence, +2 defense or +4% movement. Existing movement and Intelligence caps remain. Normal grace art is a quarter of its prior dimensions; Angel Graces are larger. The last chapter uses larger rainbow versions, with the final Angel Grace largest of all. Colors, floating wings and halo are preserved.

Enemy stats are fixed by chapter, never reduced to match a traveler. Boss HP = round(54 + 14c + 0.8c²), damage = 4 + 2c + floor(c²/16), armor = floor(0.7c), with c = chapter minus one. Phase two adds two damage. Miniboss and ordinary enemies have gentler matching curves. The last boss has 609 HP, 13 armor and 64 base damage: its first hit defeats a fresh 24-HP traveler; a developed mixed build survives and its upgraded weapon penetrates the armor. Projectiles retain fair edge warnings and dodge gaps. Best weapons provide +28 attack; best head/chest/leg gear together provides 28 defense before grace gifts. Rarity remains Basic/Strong/Godlike, prices 2/5/8, and later chapters improve RNG odds.

E is the remappable Collect action for nearby keys, coins, glowberries and equipment. Walking or clicking an item does not collect it. Reading a landmark does not take its key. Mobile has a Collect button, enabled in range. Left click/tap moves, right click swings, I opens the ten-slot pack. Collection, repeat prevention, full bags and major-encounter keys persist through saves. Two loose coins and two berries per eight campaign rooms now yield fifty of each across the campaign.

Save format remains 4 and uses the same local keys/cloud table. campaignRooms:10 marks the expanded layout. Old six-room positions map [0,1,2,3,4,5] to [0,1,2,3,5,9]. Old midpoint encounters move to room 5. Story memories, seen rooms, puzzle flags, kills, keys, dropped loot, checkpoints and expedition return points migrate once. Old chapter gifts remain the regular grace gift; old rested entrance anchors remain valid until a new grace is rested at. New Game resets campaign progress as before. Authentication, cloud configuration and verified KingKippy admin authorization remain unchanged.

Validation: 200 unique names/layouts and live renders; all 20 puzzles; all 20 minibosses and 20 bosses via both outcomes; 199 backward links; six shops; 40 grace choices with stronger Angel effects and exact-room respawn; all eighteen trail challenges/relics; 1,562 collision-safe object/exit routes and return paths across 218 maps; old-save idempotence; E-only pickups/scarcity; actual starter one-shot and developed-build survival. Desktop and landscape phone browser checks cover title/chooser, walking and collection, both grace types, key drops, final rainbow visuals, actual final-boss damage, help, saves and reload. Twenty chapter themes, voiced Brindle, equipment, character animation and fullscreen pixel menus remain.
